## 📖 How to Use open-higgsfield

### 🎯 Writing Effective Prompts

The quality of your generated images and videos depends heavily on your prompt. Here are some tips:

- **Be specific**: Instead of "a dog," try "a golden retriever puppy sitting on a beach at sunset, wearing a red bandana."
- **Include style cues**: Add words like "photorealistic," "watercolor painting," "3D render," "anime style," or "black and white."
- **Describe the mood**: Words like "serene," "dramatic," "whimsical," or "moody" help guide the AI.
- **Mention lighting and composition**: "Soft golden hour lighting," "close-up shot," "wide angle view."

### 🎛️ Understanding Model Settings

Each AI model has its own set of adjustable options. Common settings include:

- **Resolution**: Higher resolution means sharper images but takes longer to generate.
- **Steps**: More steps can improve quality but increase generation time.
- **Guidance Scale**: Higher values make the AI follow your prompt more strictly; lower values allow more creative freedom.
- **Seed**: A fixed seed produces the same result every time. Change it for variety.

Don't worry if these terms are unfamiliar. The default settings work well for most users, and you can experiment as you become more comfortable.

### 🗂️ Managing Your Gallery

Your gallery is your personal collection of everything you've generated. From here you can:

- **Preview** any image or video by clicking on it.
- **Download** your favorite creations to your computer.
- **Delete** unwanted items to keep your gallery organized.
- **Search** by prompt text or date to find specific creations.
- **Export** multiple files at once for easy sharing.
